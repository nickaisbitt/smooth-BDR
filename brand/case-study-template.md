# Case study template

Rule first: only claims the audit record can source. No placeholders. If a
field has no real value yet, the case study doesn't ship.

---
title: "<Client>: <one-line outcome>"
client: "<Business name>"
sector: "Heating"
engagement: "<Map only / Map + Diagnostic / Map + Diagnostic + Build>"
timeline: "<e.g. Map Mar 2026, build Apr 2026>"
---

## Starting point

Two sentences. What the business looked like before: size, how customer
contact ran, what hurt.

## What we looked at

Three bullets, the real workflow. Name the actual tools and hand-offs, not
categories. ("Quotes lived in Dave's inbox, retyped into a spreadsheet on
Fridays.")

## What we did

Three to five sentences, concrete. What was built or changed, in plain
English. No tool names unless the client would use them.

## What changed

Two metrics with baselines and an honest caveat. ("Booking confirmations
went from same-day phone tag to automatic, saving roughly 6 hours a week.
Payback at current volume: about 3 months. Quiet weeks save less.")

## In their words

One or two sentences, a real quote, with name and role. Anonymous quotes
("Owner, 4-person business") are placeholders, not proof. Get permission.

## Facts box

- Cost: <fixed fee actually paid>
- Payback: <argued months, with the assumption stated>
- Keep: <what the client owns outright>
