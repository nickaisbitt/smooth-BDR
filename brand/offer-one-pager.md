# The offer: The Map, then a fixed-fee fix

Hi, I'm Nick. I run Smooth AI Consultancy from Canada. I work with heating
firms - furnace, boiler, forced-air. No deck, no jargon, no retainer to
find out if any of this is worth doing.

## How it works

1. **You book a Map call.** 30 minutes on screenshare. We sketch how a job
   really flows in your firm: the missed call, the quote, the chase, the
   review that never gets asked for.
2. **You get the Map outline.** On the call: a plain-English outline of
   where the work leaks - the loop, the hours, the rough math (USD, ranges
   not promises) - a recording, and a 90-day next step you can run
   yourself. You keep it even if you never hire me.
3. **Want the leaks proven first? The paid diagnostic.** I mystery-shop
   your enquiry flow and hand you every leak with screenshots in 48 hours.
   Fixed $250-$500 USD, quoted before we start, credited in full to a
   build if you go ahead.
4. **If the math works, I quote the fix.** A fixed fee for a finished
   outcome, scoped to the workflow, not hours. You know before you commit.
5. **I build it, then I stay for the unsexy part.** Live in 14 days. 60
   days after go-live I watch, fix edge cases, and nudge adoption. Then
   you're not locked in. No forever retainer, no hostage docs.

## The ladder

**Step 1: The Map (free).** The outline level and the whole point. 30-min
call, recording, rough math, yours to keep. If I'm wrong for you, I say so
on that call.

**Step 2: The diagnostic ($250-$500 USD, 48 hours, credited).** The
proof level: every leak with screenshots and honest hours math. Credited
in full to the build if you proceed.

**Step 3: One Fix Build.** One workflow, fixed once: missed-call textback,
quote follow-up, or the review engine. Live in 14 days. Fixed quote after
the Map. Most single-workflow builds land between $2,000 and $12,000 USD
depending on how tangled it is and how much we reuse what you already pay
for. Includes the 60-day watch, edge-case fixes, and adoption nudges -
not sold separately. Around day 45, if the numbers look good, I'll ask
one thing: the name of one other heating-firm owner who'd want the same
honesty. No pressure, no discount-for-leads scheme. Just the ask, once.

## What I don't do

No hourly billing. No sprints you can't track. No selling you something
you don't need. Low-volume workflows rarely pay back, and I'll call that
out in the Map rather than after an invoice. I'd rather lose the sale than
build something that won't pay for itself.
