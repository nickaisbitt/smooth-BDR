# Smooth AI Consultancy: logo usage

Source: `brand/logo-mark.svg` (Heritage Stroke, locked 2026-09). Ink
rounded square `rx=11` with one paper S stroke (`#1A1A1E` on `#FFFCF8`,
5.6 width on the 48 grid, round caps). Full rules in `brand/identity.md`.

## Rules for outreach

1. **Cold openers and follow-ups: no image logo.** Image attachments add
   spam weight and the sender agent writes text-only bodies. Identity in
   cold mail comes from the wordmark + signature, not pixels.
2. **Reply-stage HTML wrapper (brand/email-wrapper.html):** mark appears
   once at 40px, `alt="Smooth AI Consultancy"`, linked to
   https://smoothaiconsultancy.com. Paper background `#FFFCF8`, tape
   rule `#E6D77A`, clay link `#8A4F1F`.
3. **Never** recolor the tile, add gradients/glows, use the old violet
   `#863bff`, or place it on photo backgrounds. Clearspace is half the
   tile width; minimum 16px digital.
4. **Secondary domain** (smoothaigrowth.com) carries no logo pages; its
   only brand surface is the wordmark in the email signature.
